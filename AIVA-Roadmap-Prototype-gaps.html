01 / METHOD

How to read this

The roadmap describes capabilities in engineering terms. The prototype answers a different question: what should this feel like, and what content model does it need? Where the prototype demonstrates the user-facing behaviour a capability exists to produce, it's marked Represented — even though the engineering underneath is simulated.

Represented

The behaviour is built and demonstrable. Evidence names the function, the personas, and a command you can type.

Partial

Some of the behaviour exists; a specific piece is missing. The gap is stated exactly.

Not represented

Nothing in the prototype demonstrates it. A concrete interaction is proposed that would.
